## Batch decision

This release makes project knowledge trustworthy under change. It covers source/evidence updates, missing origins, conflicts, stale accepted knowledge, access-state changes, retained-content lifecycle, export, and deletion using the F122/F123 owner/version contract.

Production connector authorization and revocation remain owned by F096 in `v0.2.3`. F127 defines and tests the fail-closed evidence-owner access adapter that every local project-source, task attachment, Result snapshot, browser/web capture, and future connector must satisfy before its content can participate in Partner retrieval.

## FEATURE_127: Knowledge Freshness, Conflict, and Access Integrity

### User outcome

When a local source changes, disappears, conflicts with another source, or becomes unavailable, Partner identifies affected knowledge and answers conservatively. Users receive an actionable review item instead of silently continuing with stale or unsupported claims.

### Dependency and impact model

- Maintain bounded links from evidence owner/version/locator to knowledge claim/page version and from a superseded page to its replacement.
- A new evidence/source version computes changed/removed evidence units and marks dependent active knowledge for review without rewriting it.
- Impact records include cause, affected claims/pages, old/new versions, severity, detected time, and proposed action.
- Refresh remains immutable: old citations stay attached to their exact old evidence owner/version.

This dependency model supports provenance and maintenance. It is not F075 semantic knowledge-graph retrieval.

### Freshness and conflict behavior

- Freshness uses evidence/content version, parser/index generation, availability state, and explicit review time rather than page `updatedAt` alone.
- Conflict detection proposes a review group when two permitted evidence versions or active pages make incompatible claims within the same subject/time scope.
- Partner answers cite both sides, distinguish dates/authority, label uncertainty, and avoid silently choosing a winner.
- Users resolve by accepting a replacement decision, recording scope/time differences, or keeping an explicit unresolved conflict.
- New evidence can reopen a resolved conflict with a new audit event.

### Access-state contract

Every evidence-owner/project-source adapter exposes four orthogonal facts for an exact owner/version:

- `liveAvailability`: `current`, `stale`, `missing`, or `deleted`;
- `originAccess`: `authorized`, `revoked`, or `unknown`;
- `retainedSnapshotUse`: `permitted`, `audit-only`, or `restricted`;
- `retainedContentAvailability`: `present`, `missing`, `forgotten`, or `corrupt`;
- a bounded non-secret reason code and observation time.

A missing or deleted live origin blocks live-origin reads, refresh, and current-pointer advancement. It does not by itself revoke an exact retained version; retrieval/citation content remains possible only when `retainedSnapshotUse=permitted` and `retainedContentAvailability=present`. `originAccess=unknown` fails closed for every origin read/refresh just like `revoked`, while the independent retained policy may still permit an exact local snapshot.

Revocation blocks new origin reads and refresh immediately. A retained snapshot may remain reusable only when its recorded policy sets `retainedSnapshotUse=permitted` and retained content is `present`. `audit-only` exposes bounded opaque citation identity, tombstone, reason, and impact metadata without title, URL, local path, external resource/account identity, excerpt, inferred claim, preview, export, or retrieval content. `restricted` excludes content from retrieval, preview, export, artifacts, traces, and diagnostics. Derived knowledge moves to `restricted` whenever continued use would expose evidence no longer permitted by its provenance/retention policy.

`Forget retained evidence` writes `retainedContentAvailability=forgotten` and a bounded tombstone without falsifying the live-origin or authorization axes. The same exact version cannot silently rehydrate; a later explicit authorized refresh/reacquisition creates a new immutable version and audit event even when the bytes match.

Reauthorization never assumes that a new identity/account is equivalent to the previous one.

Local missing/removal and project access changes ship here. F096 must map connector revocation, account switching, and snapshot provenance into the same contract before enabling connector-backed retrieval.

### Retention, export, and deletion

The product exposes four different commands:

- `Detach from task`: remove only the task selection.
- `Remove from project materials`: after an impact preview, transition only the F122 `ProjectMaterialRelation` to its retained removed/tombstone state and end future project-grounded eligibility; never detach an existing task selection or delete the target owner, local file, or external resource.
- `Forget retained evidence`: remove eligible immutable snapshot content, chunks/vectors, caches, and raw capture content; write `forgotten` plus bounded tombstones and impact receipts for surviving citations/results/knowledge.
- `Delete underlying file`: use the governed Files/F113/F114 path. Deleting an external provider resource is outside this read-only contract.

No command silently cascades into generated Results or active project knowledge. A removed material relation retains its lifecycle/target tombstone and only ends future project eligibility; surviving target and frozen task refs separately render permitted, unavailable, audit-only, or restricted from the current four-axis decision. Retention exceptions are explicit and never presented as deletion success. Project export preserves portable permitted material/evidence manifests, accepted knowledge, versions, citations, conflicts, and integrity hashes without credentials or inaccessible excerpts.

### Updates inbox

The long-lived Updates inbox belongs to the project-level material and knowledge management surface reached from F130's context drawer. It groups changed/missing material or evidence owners, failed jobs, stale knowledge, conflicts, restricted content, and proposed refreshes. Batch actions are bounded, previewed, and return per-item results.

F130 keeps task-local integrity facts in the surfaces where they help the current decision:

- **Context** shows the freshness, availability, and access/content state of current-task evidence and selectable project materials, with a direct route to project management for repair or review.
- **Results** marks the selected result's cited evidence as stale, unavailable, forgotten, restricted, or conflicting and links to the exact affected citation, evidence owner/version, or review item.
- **Process** shows only failures and recovery actions for ingestion, retrieval, or integrity checks performed by the current run. It is not long-lived material/evidence-health history.

When an adapter becomes unavailable or revoked, its add/picker capability may disappear, but existing references do not vanish:

- **Context** retains current-task and project-material rows with safe typed availability/access state and task detach actions.
- **Results** retains result identity and safe evidence-state metadata; permitted snapshots may still open, while audit-only/restricted snapshots expose only the allowed reason, tombstone, and remediation route.
- **Process** shows only the current run's access failure, cancellation, or reauthorization action.
- **Project management** owns retained snapshot inspection, removal, forget impact previews, and long-lived Updates.

F127 does not add a fourth Partner rail tab, restore a permanent Sources rail, or place the full Updates inbox in the conversation. `Results / Process / Files` remain F130's stable top-level tabs.

### Acceptance

- Changed, renamed, deleted, missing, access-restricted, conflicting, partially supported, retained, forgotten, corrupt, reacquired, and exported project-source/task-attachment/result-snapshot scenarios have deterministic tests.
- Source refresh never rewrites an accepted page or redirects an old citation to different content.
- Restricted evidence cannot leak through FTS5, vectors, caches, full-context packs, answer traces, previews, exports, artifacts, or diagnostics.
- Impact propagation is idempotent and crash/restart safe without duplicate review items.
- Conflict resolution preserves original evidence and accepted resolution history.
- A deterministic mock connector proves the adapter contract; F096 remains responsible for production connector auth/revocation acceptance.
- Context, Results, and Process project only their defined task-local integrity facts; the long-lived Updates inbox remains reachable from project management without creating another Partner rail tab.
- The full `liveAvailability x originAccess x retainedSnapshotUse x retainedContentAvailability` matrix has deterministic retrieval, preview, citation, export, and diagnostics tests, including `originAccess=unknown` and forbidden silent rehydration.
- Revoking an adapter hides its remote-origin picker and blocks remote selection/refresh, while a retained exact version may still be deliberately selected only when retained use is `permitted`, retained content is `present`, integrity passes, and the current owner/project policy allows it; existing task/result references remain typed unavailable, audit-only, restricted, or permitted-retained rows.
- Detach, remove-from-project, forget-retained-evidence, and delete-underlying-file cannot invoke one another implicitly.

## Release acceptance

- Change a source supporting an active decision and receive a precise stale review item while the old version remains auditable.
- Ask a question covered by conflicting sources and receive a conflict-aware answer with both citations.
- Restrict evidence and prove its content disappears from every retrieval route/cache while safe tombstone state remains; separately remove a project-material relation and prove it no longer participates in future project-grounded recall without silently deleting a permitted task snapshot or citation.
- Export the permitted project-knowledge set, exercise `Forget retained evidence` with a complete redacted impact receipt, and archive knowledge through F126 as a separate action; no undefined bulk knowledge delete or cross-owner cascade is implied.

## Rollout and rollback

- Gate impact propagation, automatic stale state, conflict detection, and Updates inbox independently.
- Access exclusion and revocation/restriction handling are fail-closed and cannot be disabled by a feature rollback.
- Rollback disables automatic maintenance actions while preserving readable permitted versions, citations, exports, and audit evidence.
- `v0.1.47` is reserved for freshness, conflict, deletion, retention, cache invalidation, and migration regressions.
